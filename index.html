Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: open a support request (a free GitHub account is needed to post). Please include your device model and iOS version.

Frequently asked

I bought the full game and it is locked again.
Open the unlock screen (the FULL GAME line on the title, or any locked level or circuit) and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. The App Store restores the purchase to any device signed in with the same Apple Account, including Family Sharing members.

What is free?
Career levels 1 and 2, the brakes upgrade, quick races on Downtown Loop, Sunset Speedway and Harbor Circuit, every online duel, the rankings and the Garage. One purchase unlocks the rest for good.

Online duels need Game Center.
Duels, invites and rankings run on Apple's Game Center. Sign in from the ONLINE button; the game never asks at launch.

How do I reset my career?
On the career screen, press and hold the wallet until the ring fills, then confirm. The purchase and the champion star are kept.
