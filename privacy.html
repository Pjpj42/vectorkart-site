Privacy Policy

VectorKart for iOS · effective 7 November 2026

The short version

VectorKart does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Everything the game saves (your settings, career progress and race in progress) stays on your device.

Game Center

Online duels, invitations and rankings use Apple's Game Center. When you choose to sign in, Apple handles your identity and shows your Game Center nickname to the players you race. VectorKart receives only your Game Center player identifier and nickname for the duration of a match or a leaderboard query and does not send them anywhere else. Game Center is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

In-app purchase

The one-time Full Game unlock is processed by Apple's App Store. VectorKart never sees your payment details. The purchase record is stored on your device and can be restored from the App Store on any device signed in with the same Apple Account.

Children

VectorKart is rated for everyone and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact
